Questions

Frequently asked

How is this different from other lead gen tools? ▼
Most lead gen tools are general-purpose contact databases. LeadPipeline is built specifically for local lead gen — it scrapes businesses by location and category, then rates their website quality so you know who actually needs help. It's not just data, it's a sales strategy.
What does "site quality" actually measure? ▼
We analyze each website for content depth, visible contact information, contact page presence, copyright freshness, tech stack, and online booking features. Sites are rated terrible, poor, average, or decent — so you can focus on the ones that need you most.
Do I need technical skills to use this? ▼
No. If you can click buttons and download a CSV, you can use LeadPipeline. The interface walks you through each step — scrape, enrich, review, export. No code, no API keys, no command line.
Can I use this outside the US? ▼
Yes. Google Places covers businesses worldwide. Just change the target city to any location — London, Toronto, Sydney, wherever your market is.
What happens when I run out of credits? ▼
You can upgrade to a higher plan anytime, or wait until your next monthly renewal. Free tier credits are one-time — once they're used, you pick a plan to keep going. Starter, Pro, and Agency credits reset on your billing date.
How many leads can I get with my credits? ▼
It depends on your workflow. Scrape-only uses 1 credit per business (Free tier = ~500 businesses). Full enrichment with emails uses 3-5 credits per business (Free tier = ~100-150 enriched leads). Most users find the Free tier enough to close their first deal before upgrading.
